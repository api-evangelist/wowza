What’s New In Wowza Streaming Engine 4.11? Wowza Streaming Engine 4.11 upgrades its WebRTC implementation across ingest, signaling, and network traversal. The release adds WHIP and WHEP support, full ICE candidate generation and connectivity checks, and configurable STUN and TURN servers.
