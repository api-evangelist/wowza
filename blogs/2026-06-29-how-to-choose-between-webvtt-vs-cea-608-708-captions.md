This article compares two caption formats for video streaming: CEA-608/708 captions, which are embedded within video tracks and work well for broadcast delivery, and WebVTT, which travels as separate text files suited for web and mobile playback. It explains that the right format depends on where the captions live and which player consumes them, and notes that Wowza Streaming Engine can convert between formats during packaging.
